    Questions

    What can you automate?

    Anything repetitive: lead follow-ups, CRM updates, invoices, email and WhatsApp replies, reports and more. If a person does it the same way every time, it can usually be automated.

    What is a voice agent?

    An AI that answers or makes phone calls in a natural voice. It can book appointments, qualify leads and answer questions, day and night.

    Do I need technical knowledge?

    No. You tell me the problem in plain words and I handle the building, testing and setup.

    Who is the AI coaching for?

    Beginners, teams and young learners. We go hands-on, from your first prompt to a real project you ship.

    How long does a project take?

    Simple automations can go live within days. Websites and apps usually take a few weeks, depending on scope.
